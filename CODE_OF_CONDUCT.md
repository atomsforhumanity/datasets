# Code of conduct

We want this to be a welcoming place for everyone interested in better chemistry data, whatever their
background, career stage or field.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive; assume good intent; credit other people's ideas; keep
discussions focused on the science and the data.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, publishing others'
private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

This code of conduct follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Reporting:** email **datasets@afh.org**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions, and may block participants, who do not follow this code.
